Bridging real-time voice agents to traditional phone networks typically involves the headache of managing SIP trunks and media gateways. LiveKit Connectors provides a managed integration that connects rooms directly to external telephony APIs like Twilio and WhatsApp. For WebRTC developers building conversational AI, this eliminates significant operational overhead when taking voice agents beyond the browser.
